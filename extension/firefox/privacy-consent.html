Share active website domains?

AeroTrack can send the domain of the website currently active in your browser to the configured AeroTrack service. Nothing is sent until you choose Enable domain sharing.

What will be sent

  • A random ID created for this extension installation
  • The active website hostname, such as example.com
  • The time the server receives the record

What will not be sent

  • Full URLs, paths, query text, or page titles
  • Page content, cookies, searches, or previous history
  • Incognito tabs or a hardware serial/device identifier

You can turn this off later from AeroTrack Settings. Authorized service administrators may access these records only to operate the disclosed active-domain record feature. Flight lookup and the optional web-search field remain separate controls.
